About the Hotel

Panna's Gateway
to Luxury Living

Nestled in Kunjvan, Panna — Hotel Shanvi Landmark is the region's premier hospitality destination. Perfectly situated just 1.5 km from the Hinauta Gate of Panna Tiger Reserve and close to Khajuraho, it offers a refined travel experience for families, business guests, and couples seeking peaceful luxury.

Whether you're a wildlife enthusiast headed to the safari, a pilgrim visiting Panna's sacred temples, or a family celebrating life's grandest moments — Shanvi Landmark combines warm hospitality with modern comfort and thoughtfully designed spaces.

  • ✦ Spacious deluxe & family rooms
  • ✦ Multi-cuisine restaurant on premises
  • ✦ Premium banquet & wedding hall
  • ✦ Kids play area & lush garden
  • ✦ 1.5 km from Panna Tiger Reserve gate
  • ✦ 41 km from Khajuraho Airport
